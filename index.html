End-to-End Pipeline

Our Quality Sourcing & Processing Loop

Every metric ton of grains or fresh produce exported under the PUREPACK label undergoes rigorous, transparent validation steps before leaving the port of Mombasa or JKIA Nairobi.

Learn Sourcing Integrity
1
Contract Agronomy Selection

Crops are monitored directly by our field experts in organic-practice farms across Eastern and Rift Valley Counties.

2
Intake Verification & Testing

Grain shipments are screened for moisture content (< 13.5%) and aflatoxin concentrations (< 10 ppb) at our dry storage bays.

3
Aspiration, De-stoning & Separations

Advanced density separators extract chaff, broken kernels, and metallic impurities for maximum seed purity.

4
Fumigation, Packing & Phyto-cert

Products are professionally fumigated, packed in bespoke containers, and processed for KEPHIS phytosanitary clearance.
