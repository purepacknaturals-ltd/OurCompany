Prices, currency, deposits, balance-payment conditions and accepted payment method are set out in writing before an order proceeds. Payment may be arranged by telegraphic transfer or, for eligible transactions, letter of credit.

Delivery and Incoterms

The agreed Incoterm, named port or destination, shipment plan and delivery responsibility are stated in the relevant commercial document. FOB, CFR/C&F or CIF arrangements may be used by agreement.

Documentation and compliance

Documentation requirements are confirmed according to the product and destination. Buyers remain responsible for ensuring that their import, market-access and local regulatory requirements are understood and met.

Any change, cancellation or claim should be communicated promptly and in writing. The applicable sales contract or commercial agreement will govern the specific process and any agreed remedies.
