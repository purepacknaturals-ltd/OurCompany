About PurePack Naturals Limited

An aligned agricultural sourcing and export system for international buyers.

PurePack Naturals Limited is a Nairobi-based agricultural sourcing, value-addition, packaging, logistics and export company.

Our background

Built on trade, sourcing, logistics and technology expertise.

The company was founded by professionals with expertise in international trade, agricultural sourcing, logistics and information technology. We source from trusted farmers, producers and supply partners across Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania.

Our portfolio includes fresh fruits, vegetables, root crops, pulses, spices and herbs, as well as value-added products in powdered, ground, dried and flake forms.

Our purpose

To help international buyers access a well-coordinated, transparent and dependable agricultural sourcing system in East Africa.

Our mandate

To connect East African farmers and agricultural producers with profitable local and international markets through responsible supply-chain coordination.

Our objective

To become a trusted sourcing and export partner for natural agricultural products from East Africa.

Our service approach

Every stage aligned around the buyer’s requirements.

From verified sourcing to export-ready delivery, we keep every stage clear and coordinated.

Who we serve

Flexible support for trade and food businesses.

We serve importers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, food manufacturers, hospitality businesses, supermarkets, franchise partners and institutional buyers.

  • Small and large quantities
  • Single-product and mixed-product orders
  • Bulk, wholesale, trial and regular supply contracts
  • Custom product specifications and packaging sizes
  • Private-label and buyer-branded packaging discussions
Markets we serve

Supporting local, regional and international buyers.

Our target markets include the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, China, Türkiye, Iran, Pakistan, India and GCC countries.
