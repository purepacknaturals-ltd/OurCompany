Facility & Field

From the field to export-ready handling.

Our work connects agricultural sourcing, product checks, careful packing and practical dispatch coordination.

A closer look at our workflow

Built around responsible sourcing and buyer-ready preparation.

These stages show how we support a clear journey from the farm and supply network through to packing and export coordination.

01 · Field sourcing

Working closely with supply partners.

Product discussions begin with availability, harvest timing, quality expectations and the needs of the intended market.

02 · Product checks

Reviewing product condition and consistency.

Samples and product lots are checked to support clear conversations about the specifications a buyer requires.

03 · Packing preparation

Preparing goods for their next journey.

Packing formats, labelling needs and order quantities are coordinated around the product and customer programme.

04 · Dispatch coordination

Keeping the export conversation moving.

We help align commercial details, documents and logistics arrangements for a more organised shipment process.
